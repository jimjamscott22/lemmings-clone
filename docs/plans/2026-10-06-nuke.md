# Plan: Nuke

Goal: let the player end a hopeless run, as in the original game, instead of waiting out stuck lemmings or
restarting. It also closes the gap noted in the levels plan: a lemming that can never leave (a non-climber
pacing before a wall) used to keep a level from ever finishing.

## Behaviour

- **Press `K` or the HUD button twice.** The first press arms it (the button reads "Sure? Press again" for
  `NUKE_CONFIRM_TIME`, 3 s); the second fires it. It can't be undone, and a stray key shouldn't end a good run.
  The timer is real time, so it also counts down while paused.
- **Everyone in play gets the Bomber's fuse.** `Lemming.lightFuse()` starts the usual `BOMB_FUSE` countdown, shown
  above each head. They keep walking until it goes off, and each explosion blasts the terrain around it
  (so a nuke can still free someone, and can still drop a crowd off a ledge).
- **Anyone who reaches the exit first is saved**, as with a single Bomber: reaching the exit defuses the fuse. So
  nuking a level you've already won finishes it early without throwing the result away.
- **Lemmings still in the hatch never come out** and count as lost.
- Lemmings already exiting or splatting are left alone, and anyone with a fuse keeps the one they have.
- **The level ends as usual** once the last lemming is gone: `Crowd.finished` is true, and the normal result panel
  shows (won only if enough were saved). Retry (`R`) is the way back.

## Design

- `Crowd.nuke()` does the simulation part, headless and idempotent. `Crowd.nuked` tells the HUD.
- `NukeSwitch` (`tools/`) is the DOM-free two-press confirmation, so it is unit-tested without a browser.
- `Game` owns one `NukeSwitch` per `Session` (so a restart or new level starts disarmed), handles `K`, and ignores
  the press once the level is over, the level select is open or the crowd is already nuked. The HUD button calls
  the same `pressNuke`.
- `Hud.setNuke("ready" | "armed" | "done")` drives the button label and style.

## Tests

- `Crowd.test.ts`: release stops and the hatch counts as lost, every lemming goes off after the fuse, an
  exit-bound lemming is still saved, it's idempotent, nuking before anyone is out ends the level, and level 6
  with no Climbers (which could never end) finishes after a nuke.
- `NukeSwitch.test.ts`: arm, confirm, expiry, and needing two fresh presses after firing.
- Checked in headless Chromium: `K` arms, the arm expires after 3 s, two clicks fire it, countdowns appear and
  the level ends with the result panel.
